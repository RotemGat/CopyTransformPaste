COPY-TRANSFORM-PASTE

Zero-Shot Object-Object Alignment Guided by Vision-Language and Geometric Constraints

arXiv PDF Code BibTeX

Abstract

We study zero-shot 3D alignment of two given meshes, using a text prompt describing their spatial relation. We directly optimize the relative pose at test time (translation, rotation, and isotropic scale) with CLIP-driven gradients via a differentiable renderer, and augment language supervision with geometry-aware objectives: a fractional soft-ICP attachment term and a penetration loss to discourage interpenetration.

Method

Given source and target meshes and a prompt, we optimize pose parameters θ = (τ, q, s) through differentiable rendering and a vision-language loss, while enforcing contact and reducing collisions via geometric terms.

Key ingredients

  • Auto-orientation: canonicalize the target (bilateral symmetry / upright frame).
  • Text guidance: multi-view CLIP image–text similarity via differentiable rendering.
  • Fractional soft-ICP: attach only a closest vertex fraction to encourage controlled contact.
  • Penetration loss: penalize interpenetration beyond a small margin.
  • Scheduling: increase geometry weights over phases + progressive camera zoom-in.
  • Robustness: multiple random restarts + best-of-N selection.
